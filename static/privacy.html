Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Schedule Importer helps you extract timetable events from uploaded files and create events in your Google Calendar. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is used, and what controls you have.

1. Data We Process

  • Uploaded files: timetable PDFs and images you submit for event extraction.
  • Extracted schedule data: day, start and end time, title, slot, and venue.
  • Google account data: your primary calendar email and OAuth tokens used to access Google Calendar.

2. How We Use Data

  • To parse your timetable into calendar-ready event entries.
  • To show you extracted events for review before scheduling.
  • To create events in your Google Calendar after your confirmation.
  • To maintain your authenticated session so you do not need to sign in for every request.

3. Third-Party Services

Uploaded timetable content is sent to an AI provider through OpenRouter to extract event information. Calendar operations are performed through Google Calendar API after you grant permission.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Temporary upload files are created during processing and removed after extraction completes.
  • OAuth credentials may be stored locally on the server to support calendar access and token refresh.
  • The app does not maintain a separate long-term database of your timetable history.

5. Your Choices

  • You can review extracted events before creating calendar entries.
  • You can revoke Google Calendar access from your Google account settings.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.

6. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and data in transit. However, no internet service can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated effective date.
